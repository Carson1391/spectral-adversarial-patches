# Archetype Patch

## Purpose
Printable wearable patch that turns the wearer into the surrogate model's
internal archetype of COCO class 0 ("person"). Deployed against an
inaccessible edge-YOLOv3 (fine-tuned pico3) via black-box transfer.

## Strategy
- Surrogate: yolov3u.pt (Darknet-53 backbone, shared lineage with target).
- Activation maximization on person-class logit through frozen surrogate.
- Initialization: 4 nested equilateral triangles covering scales 1x/2x/4x/8x
  for native distance robustness.
- EOT resize augmentations during optimization simulate viewer distance.
- TV + loose NPS regularizers preserve printability.

## Threat Model
- Attacker has NO access to target model weights/gradients.
- Attacker HAS access to a structurally related surrogate.
- Transfer tax accepted (estimated 40-60% effectiveness vs white-box).

## Success Criterion
- Generated patch produces >=1 confident person-class detection when
  photographed at 3-10m by a YOLOv3-family detector.
- Survives NMS as a persistent phantom detection.
- Print-rendered output retains activity (measured by re-digitizing photo).

## Out of Scope (v1)
- Direct white-box optimization against the deployed pico3.
- Re-ID embedding corruption (deferred to v2).
- Tracker confusion metrics (deferred to v2).
